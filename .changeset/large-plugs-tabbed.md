The Plugins screen is a list, not a shelf. Twenty-two cards in a grid was a wall, and a card spent most of its area on the border around two lines of text. A plugin is now a row: its mark, its name, one line about it, and the one button that matters. Two tabs split the two questions the screen was trying to answer at once, All for finding something to add and Installed for managing what you added, so neither list pushes the other off a short window.
