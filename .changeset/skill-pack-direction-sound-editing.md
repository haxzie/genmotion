The bundled skill pack is rebuilt around direction: a new `direction` skill is read before any owner is chosen, `motion-language` and `sound-design` carry the house timing and mix numbers, and `video-editing` edits footage the user brings (podcasts, talking heads, trailers, launch cuts, social edits). Every existing skill is rewritten with frame-budgeted beat sheets and verifiable checks. Adds the `music` and `transcribe` capabilities.

MP4 and MOV renders now keep `+faststart` through the segment join and the audio mux, so a browser can start playing before the whole file has downloaded.
