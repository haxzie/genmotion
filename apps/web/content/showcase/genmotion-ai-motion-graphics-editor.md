A walkthrough of the GenMotion editor: describe what you want, let the agent author the scenes, then take over on the timeline — reorder, retime, tweak copy and motion — and export a video that matches the preview pixel-for-pixel.

## Direct it like a film

The point isn't a one-shot generation — it's the loop. Draft with the agent, refine by hand, and ship. This piece shows that back-and-forth in one continuous take.
