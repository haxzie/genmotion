A launch video for Flashmates, a rental marketplace, built end to end in GenMotion: what it is, who it's for, and why it's worth signing up — carried by motion rather than narration.

## Two sides, one story

A marketplace has to sell itself twice — to the people listing and the people renting. Animation lets both sides land in a single continuous piece instead of two separate pitches.
