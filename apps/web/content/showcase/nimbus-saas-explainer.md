Nimbus is a classic product explainer: a friendly voice-guided walkthrough of what the product does and why it matters. Each step of the flow gets its own scene, connected by smooth transitions that keep the story moving.

## One idea per scene

Keeping a single idea on screen at a time makes the whole thing feel effortless to follow — and it's exactly how the agent structures a draft before you refine it.
