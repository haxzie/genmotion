Pulse takes a dry quarterly update and turns it into something people actually watch to the end. Numbers count up, bars grow into place, and each stat gets a moment to land before the next arrives.

## Built for clarity

Every figure animates on beat so the eye always knows where to look. The result reads like a motion-designed report — without a motion designer in the loop.
