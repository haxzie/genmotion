Lumen is a brand sizzle reel: five short vignettes that each capture a facet of the brand, cut together into one momentum-building sequence. Consistent color and type tie the whole piece together.

## A system, not a one-off

Because the scenes share a design system, the reel feels intentional from the first frame to the last — and the same system can be reused for the next campaign.
