**Transcription** converts spoken audio into text — typically with a timestamp for each word or phrase — turning a voiceover into something searchable and editable as text rather than only as sound.

That per-word timing is also what makes automatic captions possible: once speech is transcribed, the same timestamps place caption text on the timeline in sync with the audio, without typing and timing it by hand.
