**Generative music** is composed by an AI model from a prompt — a mood, genre, tempo, or reference track — rather than recorded by musicians or licensed from a stock library. It can be generated to an exact duration, which matters when a music bed needs to land precisely at a video's length.

Like other generative audio, it sits alongside licensed and stock options rather than replacing them outright — the right choice depends on how specific the mood and instrumentation need to be.
