A **prompt** is the text instruction you give a generative model to steer its output — the subject, style, mood, or constraints you want reflected in the result. Prompting is closer to briefing a collaborator than writing code: vague prompts get generic results, and specific ones narrow the space of what the model can produce.

In GenMotion, prompting isn't a one-shot incantation — it's a conversation. You describe what you want, review the result, and give the agent notes the way you would a human editor.
