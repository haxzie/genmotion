---
term: "Voice Cloning"
description: "Generating speech in a specific person's voice from a short sample of their real voice."
faqs:
  - q: "What is voice cloning?"
    a: "Voice cloning trains or conditions a text-to-speech model on a sample of a specific person's voice, so new speech can be generated that sounds like that person rather than a generic voice."
  - q: "Is voice cloning the same as text-to-speech?"
    a: "Voice cloning is a specialized form of text-to-speech — standard TTS uses a stock voice, while cloning targets one specific, identifiable person's voice from a reference sample."
---

**Voice cloning** conditions a [text-to-speech](/glossary/text-to-speech) model on a sample of a specific person's voice, so new lines can be generated that sound like that person spoke them — rather than a generic, stock voice.

It raises real consent and identity questions that plain TTS doesn't, which is why most voice-cloning tools require verifying rights to the voice being cloned before it can be used.

See also: [Text-to-Speech (TTS)](/glossary/text-to-speech), [Lip Sync](/glossary/lip-sync).
