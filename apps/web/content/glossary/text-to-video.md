**Text-to-video** is a class of generative AI models that produce moving footage straight from a written prompt — no filming, no keyframes, no manual animation. You describe a scene and the model renders pixels frame by frame that approximate it.

The tradeoff is control: because the output is generated pixels rather than structured layers, refining a specific detail usually means re-prompting the whole clip and hoping the rest stays consistent. GenMotion takes a different approach — the agent authors an actual scene you can inspect and edit, built from [keyframes](/glossary/keyframe) and code, rather than an opaque generated clip.
