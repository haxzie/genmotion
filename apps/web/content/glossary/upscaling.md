term: "Upscaling"

faqs:
  - q: "What is AI upscaling?"
    a: "AI upscaling increases a video or image's resolution by inferring plausible fine detail that wasn't in the original, rather than simply stretching existing pixels."
  - q: "Is upscaling the same as just resizing?"
    a: "No — resizing stretches existing pixel data and gets blurry at larger sizes. Upscaling models add new, plausible detail, which is why results look sharper rather than just bigger."
---

**Upscaling** increases a video or image's resolution using a model trained to infer plausible fine detail, rather than simply stretching the existing pixels. A naive resize just makes each pixel bigger and blurrier; an upscaler adds texture and edges that look native to the higher resolution.

It's commonly used to take a generated clip up to a deliverable resolution, or to rescue lower-resolution footage for a modern [aspect ratio](/glossary/aspect-ratio) and delivery size.

See also: [Latent Space](/glossary/latent-space), [Inpainting](/glossary/inpainting).
