**Aspect ratio** is the ratio of a frame's width to its height. Common ratios include **16:9** (widescreen, standard for YouTube and desktop), **9:16** (vertical, for Stories, Reels, and TikTok), **1:1** (square, for feeds), and **4:5** (portrait, for Instagram).

Choosing the right aspect ratio up front matters because it determines how your composition is framed for its destination. Our free [Aspect Ratio Calculator](/tools/aspect-ratio-calculator) helps you find exact pixel dimensions for any ratio.
