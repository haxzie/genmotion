A **waveform** is a visual graph of an audio track's amplitude over time — taller peaks for louder moments, flat stretches for silence. It's the shape editors read to find a beat, a word, or a pause without repeatedly scrubbing and listening.

On a timeline, a waveform turns audio into something you can edit visually — snapping a cut to a beat, or checking that a [ducked](/glossary/audio-ducking) music bed actually drops under a line of dialogue.

See also: [Audio Ducking](/glossary/audio-ducking), [Loudness Normalization (LUFS)](/glossary/loudness-normalization).
