---

**Loudness normalization** adjusts an audio track's perceived volume to a standard target, measured in **LUFS** (Loudness Units Full Scale) — a unit that reflects how loud audio actually sounds to human hearing, unlike raw peak amplitude. Streaming platforms and broadcasters each target a specific LUFS level so content doesn't jump wildly in volume from one video to the next.

For a video with both a [voiceover](/glossary/text-to-speech) and a music bed, normalization is also what keeps the two in a sane relative balance rather than one drowning out the other.

See also: [Audio Ducking](/glossary/audio-ducking), [Waveform](/glossary/waveform).
