**Inpainting** regenerates a masked region of an image or video frame — removing an unwanted object, fixing a glitch, or swapping a detail — while leaving everything outside the mask untouched. It's the generative equivalent of a targeted retouch rather than a full re-generation.

Its counterpart, **outpainting**, extends the frame outward instead, generating plausible new content beyond the original edges — useful for changing an [aspect ratio](/glossary/aspect-ratio) without cropping.

See also: [Diffusion Model](/glossary/diffusion-model), [Upscaling](/glossary/upscaling).
