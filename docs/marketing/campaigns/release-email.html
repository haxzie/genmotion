GenMotion Desktop 0.6.0
Demo your product without a screen recorder
GenMotion's agent has a browser now. Open your product in it, say what you want shown, and it clicks through the flow, keeps the screens it needs, and cuts them into a demo. Nobody has to record anything, and nobody has to start over because of a fumbled click at the 40 second mark.

What's new
Real product demos
Hand it a URL and the steps you want covered. What ends up on screen is your actual product, not a mockup of it, and when a step needs a login you can take the keyboard and do that part yourself.
Your brand, down to the hex
It pulls colours and typefaces straight out of your site's stylesheet, so you stop getting videos that are almost your blue.
You can watch, and butt in
There's a Browser tab in the editor showing the page it's on. Grab the mouse any time you'd rather click something yourself.
Someone to present it, if you want one
Connect fal and ElevenLabs in the Plugins tab and the agent can put a presenter in the video: an AI avatar talking over your product, in a voice you picked. A talking-head demo with nobody on camera and nothing to book.
Start from the Figma file
Connect Figma in the Plugins tab and hand the agent a file, a frame, or the storyboard you already drew. It animates what is actually in there, your components and your styles, so you are not describing a design you have already made.
